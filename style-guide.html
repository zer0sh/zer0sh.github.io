04 // Governance Laws (Innovative UX Designer V3)

LAW.01
Zero SVG Mandate: Never author, generate, edit, or embed inline SVG markup. All geometry, indicators, and dividers are built strictly from pure CSS borders, border-radius, and whitespace.
LAW.02
Evidence-Derived Form: Form emerges from real engineering artifacts (kernel logs, sockets, CI/CD telemetry, terminal codes), never from contemporary generic SaaS templates or AI clichés.
LAW.03
Deterministic Performance: Low energy emission palette, 100% Lighthouse rating, zero heavy runtime dependencies, and strict accessibility contrast compliance.
LAW.04
Bilingual Symmetry: English and Spanish translations maintain identical structural rhythm, layout balance, and semantic precision.
